# Project Design Theme: Maya’s Cafe Website

## Theme Overview

The Maya’s Cafe website should feel like a warm, cozy, and modern digital extension of the real cafe. The visual direction should be inspired by the cafe’s handwritten signage, warm lighting, dark furniture, cream seating, wooden details, indoor plants, decorative wall elements, and relaxed neighborhood atmosphere.

The design should communicate that Maya’s Cafe is an affordable and welcoming place where customers can enjoy coffee, meals, desserts, conversations, study sessions, and quiet moments.

The website should look polished and professional without appearing too luxurious, formal, or expensive. It should remain approachable, local, youthful, and comfortable.

## Design Direction

The approved design direction is:

**Cozy Noir Café**

This design style combines:

* Warm cream and ivory backgrounds
* Espresso-black content sections
* Caramel and muted-gold accents
* Elegant serif headings
* Clean modern body text
* Handwritten decorative details
* Warm food and interior photography
* Rounded cards with subtle shadows
* Simple outlined icons
* Generous spacing and clear content hierarchy

The final interface should feel refined but still friendly and accessible to everyday customers.

## Brand Personality

The design should express the following qualities:

* Cozy
* Friendly
* Affordable
* Relaxing
* Local
* Welcoming
* Youthful
* Modern
* Casual
* Slightly trendy
* Comfortable
* Community-oriented

The website should avoid feeling corporate, overly minimalist, too luxurious, or visually experimental.

## Logo Style

Use the approved handwritten **Maya’s Cafe** logo as the main brand mark.

The logo combines:

* Handwritten script lettering
* A steaming coffee-cup illustration
* A warm, personal, and approachable visual identity

Prepare the logo in the following versions:

* Espresso logo for light backgrounds
* White logo for dark backgrounds
* Muted-gold logo for decorative use
* Transparent SVG for website implementation
* Transparent PNG as a fallback

Do not replace the logo with a standard typed version.

## Typography

### Primary Heading Font

Use **DM Serif Display** for:

* Hero headings
* Section headings
* Product names
* Promotional statements
* Review-section titles
* Large calls to action

DM Serif Display should give the website a warm, elegant café character without making it feel overly formal.

Recommended weights:

* Regular 400
* Italic 400 for occasional emphasis

### Body and Interface Font

Use **Manrope** for:

* Navigation
* Paragraphs
* Buttons
* Product descriptions
* Prices
* Contact information
* Opening hours
* Form labels
* Review details
* Small interface text

Recommended weights:

* 400 Regular
* 500 Medium
* 600 SemiBold
* 700 Bold

### Decorative Script Text

Use handwritten script only for short decorative phrases such as:

* “We’d love to see you!”
* “Made with love, served with heart.”
* “See you soon!”
* “A glimpse of our space and flavors.”

Decorative script text should never be used for paragraphs, buttons, important labels, or essential information.

## Typography Scale

### Desktop

* Hero heading: 64–72px
* Main section heading: 44–52px
* Card heading: 24–30px
* Body text: 17–18px
* Button text: 15–16px
* Small labels: 13–14px

### Mobile

* Hero heading: 42–52px
* Main section heading: 34–42px
* Card heading: 22–26px
* Body text: 17–18px
* Button text: 16px
* Small labels: minimum 14px

Mobile text should never be compressed just to fit more information. When necessary, reduce the amount of content instead of reducing readability.

## Color Distribution

The visual balance should approximately follow:

* 55% warm cream and ivory
* 25% espresso and dark brown
* 10% caramel brown
* 5% muted gold
* 5% supporting colors

The website should not be completely dark. Dark sections should be used strategically to create contrast and visual rhythm.

## Layout Style

The layout should feel spacious, organized, and easy to scan.

Recommended maximum content width:

`1200px–1240px`

Recommended horizontal padding:

* Desktop: 64–80px
* Tablet: 32–40px
* Mobile: 20–24px

Recommended section spacing:

* Desktop: 96–120px
* Tablet: 72–88px
* Mobile: 64–80px

Use an 8px spacing system throughout the website.

## Page Structure

The landing page should remain compact and include only the most important sections:

1. Header and Navigation
2. Hero
3. Featured Bestsellers
4. About and Cafe Experience
5. Gallery and Customer Reviews
6. Visit Maya’s Cafe
7. Footer

Related information should be combined instead of creating too many separate sections.

## Header and Navigation

The desktop header should include:

* Maya’s Cafe logo
* Home
* Menu
* About
* Gallery
* Reviews
* Location
* Order Now button

A slim announcement bar may display:

* Opening hours
* Dine-in availability
* Takeout availability
* Delivery availability

The desktop header should be clean, light, and spacious.

On mobile:

* Display the logo on the left
* Display a large menu button on the right
* Use a full-screen or slide-out menu
* Keep menu links large and readable
* Maintain minimum 44px touch targets

## Hero Section

The hero should use a strong photograph of the actual cafe interior.

The image should feature:

* Warm lighting
* Cream seating
* Dark tables and chairs
* Maya’s Cafe signage
* Plants and decorative details

Use a dark espresso gradient overlay on the left side to improve readability.

The hero should include:

* Small welcome label
* Large main heading
* Short description
* View Our Menu button
* Get Directions button
* Customer rating
* Opening hours
* Address
* Service availability

Suggested heading:

> Your cozy corner in Masbate

The hero should feel immersive while keeping the text readable.

## Featured Bestsellers

Display four selected products instead of the entire menu.

Each product card should include:

* Product image
* Category label
* Product name
* Short description
* Price
* Optional Bestseller or Popular badge
* Order Now button

Recommended card style:

* Ivory background
* Rounded corners
* Thin warm-gray border
* Soft shadow
* Large product image
* Muted-gold icons
* Clear price hierarchy

On mobile, cards may stack vertically or become horizontally scrollable. Product names and prices must remain easy to read.

## About and Cafe Experience

This section should combine the cafe story with the physical cafe experience.

Use:

* Short brand description
* Strong interior photograph
* Decorative handwritten phrase
* Four main experience highlights

Recommended highlights:

* Affordable Favorites
* Cozy Atmosphere
* Study, Work, Relax
* Friendly Service

The feature area may use an espresso background with cream text and muted-gold headings.

Avoid placing too much text inside each feature card.

## Gallery and Customer Reviews

The gallery should show a balanced mixture of:

* Cafe interior
* Coffee and drinks
* Meals
* Desserts
* Seating areas
* Decorative details

Use rounded image corners and a structured masonry-style layout.

The customer-review section should include:

* Overall rating
* Review count
* Star display
* Selected testimonials
* Customer name
* Visit type
* Review navigation

Keep testimonials short enough to scan quickly.

## Visit Maya’s Cafe

The Visit section should prioritize practical information.

Include:

* Address
* Opening hours
* Phone number
* Delivery information
* Parking information
* Facebook link
* Embedded map

Primary actions:

* Get Directions
* Call the Cafe
* Message on Facebook

On mobile, show only the most important contact cards:

* Address
* Opening hours
* Phone
* Delivery

Less important information may be moved into the map card, footer, or expandable details.

## Footer

The footer should use an espresso-black background.

Include:

* White Maya’s Cafe logo
* Short cafe description
* Facebook and Instagram
* Quick links
* Opening hours
* Contact information
* Service options
* Copyright
* Privacy policy
* Terms of service

On mobile, simplify the footer.

Recommended mobile footer order:

1. Logo and short description
2. Social-media links
3. Quick links
4. Opening hours
5. Contact information
6. Legal links

Avoid forcing multiple narrow columns with very small text.

## Cards

Recommended card styling:

```text
Background: #FFFDF8
Border: 1px solid #E5DDD3
Border radius: 18px
Padding: 24–32px
Shadow: soft and subtle
```

Cards should not use heavy black shadows.

## Buttons

### Primary Button

* Espresso background
* Warm-cream text
* 10–12px corner radius
* 48–54px height
* SemiBold Manrope text

### Gold Accent Button

* Muted-gold or caramel background
* Espresso or white text
* Use for Order Now or promotional actions

### Outline Button

* Transparent background
* Espresso or gold border
* Clear icon and text label

Buttons should include a subtle hover transition and visible keyboard-focus state.

## Icon Style

Use a unified custom icon system.

Icons should use:

* Rounded outlines
* 2–2.5px stroke width
* Minimal detail
* Muted-gold color on light backgrounds
* Cream or gold color on dark backgrounds
* Consistent 64×64 SVG viewBox

The custom icon library includes icons for:

* Coffee
* Location
* Clock
* Phone
* Facebook
* Instagram
* Parking
* Delivery
* Takeout
* Dine-in
* Order
* Gallery
* Reviews
* Product categories
* Decorative dividers
* Hearts and café flourishes

Use SVG assets whenever possible.

## Photography Direction

The final website should use original cafe photographs without Google Maps controls, screenshots, arrows, black borders, or interface overlays.

Recommended image treatment:

* Warm but natural color temperature
* Soft highlights
* Balanced contrast
* Realistic food colors
* Consistent cropping
* No heavy filters
* No excessive orange tint

Suggested aspect ratios:

* Hero: 16:9 or wide panoramic
* Product cards: 4:3
* Gallery images: mixed 4:3 and 3:2
* About image: 3:2 or 16:10

## Decorative Elements

Use decoration carefully.

Approved decorative details include:

* Handwritten phrases
* Coffee-cup line art
* Heart dividers
* Thin muted-gold lines
* Steam illustrations
* Subtle leaf flourishes
* Soft brush-circle backgrounds
* Light geometric textures

Decorations should support the cafe identity without competing with important content.

## Motion and Interaction

Animations should remain subtle and professional.

Recommended effects:

* Buttons move upward by 1–2px on hover
* Product images scale slightly
* Cards gain a soft shadow
* Sections fade upward when entering the viewport
* Gallery images open in a lightbox
* Review cards slide horizontally
* Header gains a shadow while scrolling

Recommended duration:

* Buttons and icons: 180–250ms
* Cards and sections: 300–450ms

Avoid dramatic parallax or constant movement.

## Mobile Design Rules

Mobile layouts must prioritize readability over information density.

Required rules:

* Minimum body text size of 16px
* Prefer 17–18px for important paragraphs
* Minimum 44px touch targets
* Large buttons
* Generous card padding
* Clear single-column layouts
* Comfortable line spacing
* Reduced number of visible links
* No overly compressed footer columns
* No tiny labels
* No dense information grids

When content does not fit comfortably, remove secondary information instead of shrinking fonts.

## Accessibility Requirements

The design should include:

* Sufficient text contrast
* Visible keyboard-focus states
* Proper heading hierarchy
* Alternative text for every image
* Text labels alongside important icons
* Minimum 44px touch targets
* Accessible navigation
* Reduced-motion support
* Clear hover, active, disabled, success, and error states

## Content Tone

The website copy should sound warm, simple, and welcoming.

Recommended language:

* Your cozy corner in Masbate
* Good food and a comfortable place to slow down
* Made with love, served with heart
* Affordable favorites for every kind of moment
* Come for the food, stay for the atmosphere
* We’d love to see you

Avoid exaggerated claims such as:

* World-class dining
* Luxury cafe experience
* The best cafe in the Philippines
* Exclusive premium destination

## Final Design Goal

The final website should look like a polished digital version of Maya’s Cafe. It should preserve the warmth and personality of the real location while improving clarity, usability, responsiveness, and customer conversion.

Every screen should make it easy for visitors to:

* Understand what Maya’s Cafe offers
* See the cafe’s food and atmosphere
* View recommended products
* Trust the business through customer reviews
* Find the location
* Contact the cafe
* Place or request an order

The design must remain consistent across desktop, tablet, and mobile while protecting readability and preserving the approved Cozy Noir Café identity.
