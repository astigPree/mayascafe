# Project Description: Maya’s Cafe Website

## Project Overview

The Maya’s Cafe Website is a responsive, modern landing page designed to establish a stronger online presence for a cozy neighborhood cafe located on Ibingay Street, Barangay Ibingay, Masbate City. The website will introduce the cafe, highlight its best-selling food and drinks, showcase the atmosphere of the physical location, display customer reviews, and make it easy for visitors to find, contact, and visit the business.

The website should reflect Maya’s Cafe as an affordable, friendly, and relaxing local destination for coffee, meals, desserts, study sessions, casual meetings, and time with friends. It must feel visually connected to the real cafe through its warm lighting, black furniture, cream seating, wooden signage, plants, decorative details, and handwritten branding.

## Main Project Goal

The primary goal is to create a short but informative landing page that helps visitors quickly understand what Maya’s Cafe offers and encourages them to take an immediate action.

The main actions are:

* View the menu
* Order food or drinks
* Get directions
* Call the cafe
* Message the cafe on Facebook
* Explore the cafe’s food and atmosphere

The landing page should avoid unnecessary length and should only present the most important information needed by potential customers.

## Target Audience

The website is intended for:

* College students looking for affordable meals and drinks
* Friends and groups searching for a cozy place to meet
* Solo customers who want a quiet place to relax
* Students and remote workers using laptops
* Families with children
* Local residents of Masbate City
* Tourists looking for a cafe nearby
* Customers interested in takeout or delivery

## Brand and Visual Direction

The approved design direction is called **Cozy Noir Café**.

It combines the warmth of a neighborhood cafe with a clean and modern digital interface. The website should look polished and professional without making Maya’s Cafe appear expensive, formal, or overly luxurious.

### Brand personality

* Cozy
* Friendly
* Affordable
* Relaxing
* Local
* Youthful
* Welcoming
* Casual
* Modern

## Responsive Design Requirements

The website must work properly across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

### Desktop

* Wide image-led layouts
* Multiple product cards per row
* Side-by-side content sections
* Full horizontal navigation

### Mobile

* Large and readable text
* Generous vertical spacing
* Single-column content where appropriate
* Large touch-friendly buttons
* Simplified information
* Stacked cards and sections
* Reduced number of footer links
* No compressed paragraphs or tiny labels

Mobile readability is more important than fitting every detail into a short screen. Less important content may be removed or placed inside expandable menus.

## UI and Component Style

The interface should use:

* Rounded cards
* Soft ivory backgrounds
* Thin warm-gray borders
* Subtle shadows
* Simple outlined icons
* Large food photography
* Clear button hierarchy
* Smooth but restrained animations
* Generous whitespace
* Consistent spacing and typography

Recommended component values:

```text
Card radius: 18px
Button radius: 10–12px
Maximum content width: 1200–1240px
Desktop section spacing: 96–120px
Mobile section spacing: 64–80px
Minimum body text: 16px
Minimum touch target: 44px
```

## Custom Assets

A complete set of custom Maya’s Cafe assets has been created in SVG and PNG formats.

The asset system includes:

* Main Maya’s Cafe logo
* Coffee-cup logo mark
* Navigation icons
* Hero icons
* Bestsellers icons

* Gallery icons
* Location and contact icons

## Accessibility Requirements

The website should include:

* Clear color contrast
* Readable font sizes
* Keyboard-accessible navigation
* Visible focus states
* Alternative text for images
* Proper heading hierarchy
* Large touch targets
* Accessible form and button labels
* Reduced-motion support
* Text labels alongside important icons

## Expected Result

The final result should be a polished, production-ready cafe website that feels like a natural digital extension of Maya’s Cafe. It should make the cafe appear welcoming, affordable, memorable, and easy to visit while preserving the identity of the real location.

The website should help customers quickly answer three questions:

1. What food and drinks does Maya’s Cafe offer?
2. What does the cafe look and feel like?
3. How can I visit, contact, or order from the cafe?
